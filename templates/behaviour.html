How Student Behaviour Is Analysed

Learning Activity Features

The clustering model uses numerical features describing recorded learning activity.

  • Assessment Average: Average recorded assessment score.
  • Assessment Count: Number of assessment records.
  • Total Clicks: Interactions with online learning materials.
  • Active Days: Number of days with recorded activity.

Clustering Method

K-Means clustering groups students according to similarities in the selected activity features.

  • Similar activity patterns are grouped together.
  • The groups summarise recorded engagement patterns.
  • The results may help identify areas where support could be useful.
  • Group membership does not determine a student's ability or future success.
Important note: These groups describe patterns in the available dataset. They are not a diagnosis or a guarantee of academic performance. The charts use the group counts supplied by your Flask application.
